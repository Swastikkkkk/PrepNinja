# Participant outcome survey (for outcome validation)

Send to all 150 study participants. Paste into Google Forms, then export responses as CSV
and save as `outcomes.csv` (columns in `templates/outcomes.csv`). Researchers fill
`final_success_probability` from platform logs, matched on participant code.

**Consent (required):** "I agree that my placement outcomes below may be used, in anonymised
form, for the PrepNinja research study. I can withdraw at any time by emailing the study team."

1. Participant code (given by the study team) *
2. Since the study ended, did you take any company online assessment (OA) or coding test? (Yes / No)
3. Did you clear at least one OA or coding test? (Yes / No / Did not take one)
4. How many technical or HR interviews were you invited to? (number)
5. How many job offers did you receive? (number)

Do not ask for names, company names or salary.
